Our story · Since 2001

It began with a rose on the streets of Izmir

Our story began on the streets of Izmir, Turkey, in 2001, with an armful of fresh flowers and a simple belief: a flower given with love should feel handpicked, never mass-produced. In Turkish, gül means rose, and we named ourselves after the flower that started it all.

From Izmir, the roses found a new home in Lahore. For years we have tied flowers for Lahore's homes, its weddings and its quiet moments, one order at a time. Today GuleGul Flowers brings that same care online, so anyone in Lahore, or anywhere in the world, can send something real to someone they love.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer same-day flower delivery in Lahore?

Yes. Bouquets, floral boxes and gift combos are delivered the same day anywhere in Lahore, and you can order at any time, day or night. The delivery charge depends on your area and is confirmed on WhatsApp.

How do I pay for my order?

We accept bank transfer and JazzCash / EasyPaisa. Payment details are shared once your order is confirmed, and overseas customers can pay by bank transfer from any country.

How much does a bouquet cost at GuleGul?

Our bouquets start from around Rs 8,500 (money bouquets) and go up to Rs 50,000 or more for grand arrangements. Rose bouquets start from about Rs 12,000, bangle bouquets from about Rs 17,500 and chocolate bouquets from about Rs 19,000. Every price is on the product page, and our flower price guide lists them all.

How early should I order kalire, gajray or a ring platter?

Floral jewelry such as kalire and gajray, and nikkah or engagement ring platters, are made to order, so please order at least 2 days ahead. Bouquets, floral boxes and gift combos can be ordered on the same day.

Can I send flowers to Lahore from abroad?

Yes. Many of our customers order from the UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, the USA, Canada and Australia. Order online or on WhatsApp, pay by bank transfer, and we deliver to the door in Lahore and send you the details.

Do you do wedding decor as well as flowers?

Yes. We do nikkah, mehndi, walima, bridal room and car decor. Designs start from Rs 30,000 (walima from Rs 80,000), and we give an exact estimate on WhatsApp once we know the design, venue and size.

Can I order a money bouquet, bangle bouquet or garland?

Yes. Money bouquets, bangle bouquets, kuchu pucho bouquets, mirror and chocolate bouquets, and bride and groom rose garlands (mala) are all part of our collection and can be ordered online or on WhatsApp.

Which areas of Lahore do you deliver to?

We deliver across Lahore, including DHA, Gulberg, Johar Town, Model Town, Cantt, Bahria Town, Park View City and Izmir Town. See our delivery page for the full list.
